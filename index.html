Find Hospital Bed Availability in Seconds

MediBed helps patients quickly search nearby demo hospitals by city, compare ICU, Oxygen, and General bed counts, spot outdated availability with automatic STALE warnings, and test temporary 2-minute demo holds.

Search by City & Bed Type

Filter demo hospitals across Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, or newly added cities by ICU, Oxygen, or General bed availability.

2

Live Hospital Updates

Hospitals can add new entries or update bed counts at any time. Every change automatically saves a fresh timestamp.

3

24-Hour STALE Protection

Any hospital data older than 24 hours automatically shows a prominent STALE warning so patients know to verify directly by phone.

4

2-Minute Demo Bed Hold

Patients can place a temporary 2-minute demo hold with a live countdown timer that prevents duplicate holds in this browser.
